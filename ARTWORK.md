# Artwork

All art was generated using the built-in ImageGen tool for Iron Contract. The web assets are in `dist/assets/`. `crater.webp`, `canyon.webp`, and `relay.webp` are the three panels of the Mars arena atlas. The infantry and weapon assets retain transparency.

## Cover

> Use case: photorealistic-natural, cinematic hero artwork for a Mars military game. Landscape widescreen composition. A close very realistic human infantry soldier positioned on RIGHT side fills the right third of frame and faces left in three-quarter profile, holding a dark futuristic rifle at the ready. Soldier wears an unmistakeably BLUE uniform with strong cobalt blue textile sleeves and navy blue tactical armor, scuffed blue sealed helmet and dark transparent visor. A second, distant soldier in similar blue armor visible among a Mars cargo outpost in soft focus on the far right; no soldiers in left half. The setting is unmistakably MARS: rusty red volcanic rock, ocher dust atmosphere and red craters under dark warm dusty sky, a distant modular colony installation. Deep muted shadows on the left give quiet space for overlaid type. Orange rust ground and blue suit are strongly contrasted. Gritty physical film texture, detailed plausible armor. No text, no graphic overlays, no logos, no blood, no injury.

## Mars arena atlas

> Generate one photoreal game environment atlas with exactly three full-bleed, equal-height widescreen 16:9 images stacked vertically without dividers (overall roughly 16:27). ALL three locations are on MARS; authentic desolate dry iron-red Martian regolith and rocky canyon terrain, no plants, oceans, or Earth. TOP THIRD: bright burnt-orange red dusty Martian crater cargo outpost from eye-level, futuristic rusty modular cargo containers left and right, wide open dirt lane foreground center, low sunlight and opal dusty horizon. MIDDLE THIRD: Martian canyon ore-transport rail depot at blue dusk, red dust and red steep cliffs, industrial floodlights and derelict rail machinery framing an open central lane. BOTTOM THIRD: Mars relay base, hard steel satellite dishes and modular industrial buildings, in a howling orange Martian dust storm, warm amber lamps, jagged red cliffs, clear open foreground. Human-scale realistic gritty game photography. Each 16:9 view is intended as an unobstructed first-person arena. NO people, no rifles, no text, no overlays, no borders, no injury.

## Blue infantry sprite

> Photoreal 2D game asset. One very realistically rendered full-body muscular adult future infantry soldier in a clearly BLUE military uniform: blue-grey woven armored tactical flight suit, rich steel-blue outer cloth covering arms and legs, cobalt blue plates across shoulders/chest/thighs, bulky blue sealed Mars combat helmet with smoke-grey visor and a thin breathing tube, black boots and gloves. Human facial proportions subtly visible through visor. Front view, holding a generic believable dark compact rifle level aiming toward camera, entire head, barrel, body and both boots contained in frame. Photograph-like real materials, textured suit, heavy worn armor, plausible rugged Mars infantry gear. On an ACTUAL transparent alpha background with no floor, no shadow behind, no scenery or studio backdrop; crisp edges for sprite cutout. Centered narrow portrait composition. Only one soldier. No text, logos or gore.

## First-person weapon

> Photoreal isolated foreground sprite only, for a gritty realistic first-person video game set on Mars. View from the player's own eyes of their compact dark gunmetal futuristic military carbine, cradled with BOTH blue pressure-suit gloved hands in proper first-person camera perspective. View along top of black rail toward muzzle pointing away from viewer, slightly toward upper left. Rifle and blue sleeves extend diagonally from the lower right corner toward the upper left quarter; blue-gray sleeve fabric, hard carbon and navy polymer. Close-up orientation, very faithful weapon proportions and believable real-world materials. Wide 4:3 landscape, crop forearms along bottom and right, keep foregrip and muzzle within frame. Transparent alpha background, NO sky or scenery, no ground plane, no soldier face/body, no muzzle flash, no markings, no letters, no interface.
